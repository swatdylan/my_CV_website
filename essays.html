Synthetic Synapses and Virtual Minds: Challenging the Biological Necessity of Consciousness

The Red Stone Journal: An Undergraduate Journal of NUS Philosophy, Vol. 5(1)

Abstract

This paper challenges biological organicism, the view that consciousness requires a biological substrate. It argues against that claim using two arguments: the Brain-Computer Interface (BCI) Argument and the Virtual Mind Argument.

ESSAY OCT 2024

On the Paradox of Confirmation

Dylan Ang Kai Hao

Summary

An explanation and defence of Hempel’s paradox of confirmation: taking Nicod’s Criterion and the Equivalence Condition together, any object that is not a raven or is black confirms that all ravens are black. The essay accepts this conclusion and answers an objection to the Equivalence Condition.
